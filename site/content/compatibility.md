## Other mods' benches

If you have any of these mods, their benches accept the linkables too. You don't need to change your load order for it.

{{ mod_list() }}

"Workshop" means the workshop machines from the [Workshop](workshop.html) page. "Lab equipment" is the still, rotary evaporator and tabletop centrifuge, stove and tube rack.

[Power linkables](power.html) need no list, because they find generators by how they make power rather than by name.

## Settings

On RimWorld 1.7 with [Vanilla Expanded Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2023507013) active, the mod adds a Buildings page to its mod settings. For each building you can change:

- its bonus to work speed, research speed or power output, up to 1000%,
- the laser engraver's quality bonus, up to 6 levels,
- its cost in steel, plasteel, components and advanced components,
- how much power it uses.

Only the values you change are saved, so the mod's own values still apply to everything else, even after an update changes them.

## Tooltips

On RimWorld 1.7, when you select a bench or generator, the build buttons for its linkables show a tooltip: what the building does, where it has to stand, and how many are linked out of the maximum, for example "Linked: 1 / 2".
