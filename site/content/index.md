## What the mod does

In vanilla RimWorld, a tool cabinet next to a workbench makes your crafters faster. Even More Linkables adds a lot more buildings that work the same way, in three groups:

- [Workshop](workshop.html) machines, such as a CNC machine, a lathe or a robot arm, make crafting benches work faster. The laser engraver raises the quality of what the bench makes.
- [Laboratory](laboratory.html) equipment, from a tabletop microscope to a server rack, makes research benches faster. A few pieces also link to the drug lab and biofuel refinery.
- [Power](power.html) linkables, such as transformers and cooling towers, raise the output of generators, including generators from other mods.

You unlock them with nine projects on their own Linkables tab in the [research](research.html) screen.

Each page lists which benches every building links to, how many of it one bench can use, and whether it has to touch the bench or can stand nearby.
